### The "Offshore Broking platform" — what it actually does
- **"Offshore broking" = chartering and S&P of offshore-support vessels (OSVs)** for the oil & gas and offshore-wind industries — NOT offshoring of labor. Vessel types: **PSV** (Platform Supply Vessel), **AHTS** (Anchor Handling Tug Supply), **MPSV/CSV** (construction/subsea), crew-transfer vessels, and **rigs**. SSY's offshore desk runs from Aberdeen, Dubai, Hamburg, Kristiansand and Oslo; Head of Offshore is **Frank Holck** (ex-F3 Offshore). SSY already publishes a weekly Offshore Spot Market Update and has an "SSY Offshore App."
- **What a purpose-built offshore platform needs to do:** manage **vessel/tonnage lists and positions** (often via AIS data), **cargo/requirement lists**, **fixtures** (concluded charters), **day-rate benchmarking** (OSV rates are quoted as $/day), **charter-party / recap generation**, **laytime/demurrage tracking**, and **market intelligence/analytics**. Offshore differs from dry/tanker broking: it is a smaller, more relationship-driven market with day-rate (not voyage) economics, project-based demand (drilling campaigns, wind-farm O&M), and is historically run on **Excel + email** with few purpose-built tools.
- **Market context the candidate should know:** the OSV market was strong in 2023–24 but **softer in 2025** — Veson Nautical reports "PSV utilisation has eased to c.78%, while AHTS has remained closer to c.80%," and Fearnley Offshore Supply notes "composite dayrates... declined by approximately 16% for PSVs and 14% for AHTS vessels" versus 2024. A **rebound is forecast from late 2026**, driven by an ageing fleet, tight newbuild supply, and offshore-wind demand. This matters: SSY is building tooling for a sector it expects to grow.

### Competitive landscape
- **Clarksons (Clarkson PLC)** is the clear #1 and most tech-advanced — its **Sea/ platform** (spun out, now Maritech) dominates charter-party/recap management. Other majors: **BRS Group** (Geneva, ~420 staff, owns AXSMarine), **Braemar**, **Arrow**, **Howe Robinson**, **Ifchor Galbraiths**, **Fearnleys**, **Poten & Partners**, **Gibson**, **MB Shipbrokers** (ex-Maersk Broker).
- **Key competitive-tech story:** In February 2025, **SSY joined Arrow, Gibson, Howe Robinson and Ifchor Galbraiths to launch "Ocean Recap"** — a charter-party/recap platform (built with Signal Ocean, CEO Jeroen Wolthuis) explicitly created to break Sea/'s **monopoly** and address **data-ownership/security** concerns (Ocean Recap doesn't claim ownership of uploaded data). Per the founding partners' release, "Ocean Recap has already demonstrated its value during rigorous internal testing, producing over 1,000 Charter Parties for more than 75 Clients across both the Tanker and Dry markets" over an 18-month period, with a full release planned for mid-2025. Wolthuis called it "a defining moment for the maritime industry." **This is the single most important competitive-dynamics talking point.**
- SSY also holds a **minority stake in Signal Ocean** (a data-led chartering analytics platform; partnership dates to 2019). Third-party chartering tools to show awareness of: **Veson (IMOS/Shipfix), Kpler, AXSMarine, Sea/.** There is a genuine **technology arms race** in shipbroking around data, AI email-parsing, and workflow digitisation.

### Industry context & terminology
- **2025–26 maritime themes:** decarbonization & IMO regulations (FuelEU, 2030/2050 targets), alternative fuels (**LNG, ammonia, methanol**), geopolitical disruption (**Red Sea/Houthi attacks rerouting via Cape of Good Hope; Panama Canal drought**), sanctions/"dark fleet," and digitisation/AI.
- **Must-know vocabulary:** *fixture* (a concluded charter deal), *charter party* (the contract; standard forms incl. GENCON, NYPE, BALTIME), *recap* (recapitulation of agreed terms), *laytime* (allowed loading/discharge time), *demurrage* (penalty for exceeding laytime) / *despatch* (reward for beating it), *TCE* (Time Charter Equivalent — the key voyage-profitability metric), *FFA* (Forward Freight Agreement — freight derivative), *Baltic Exchange* (publishes the BDI and route indices; SSY brokers are panellists), *S&P* (sale & purchase), *AIS* (vessel-tracking signals), *bunkers* (fuel), *BIMCO* (issues standard contracts). Vessel classes: Handysize/Supramax/Panamax/Capesize (dry bulk); Aframax/Suezmax/VLCC (tankers); PSV/AHTS/MPSV (offshore).
