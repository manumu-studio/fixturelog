The smartest questions for you to ask are these:

- **How do brokers use the Offshore platform during a live day of work—what are the highest-frequency workflows today?**
- **What are the core entities and sources of truth in the system: vessels, rigs, positions, requirements, fixtures, clients, contracts, or market data feeds?**
- **Where does the platform currently save users the most time, and where is the biggest manual bottleneck still left?**
